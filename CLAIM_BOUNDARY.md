# Claim boundary

This repository is a claim-bounded reduced-surrogate research package for **Socio-Traffic Thermodynamics (STT)**.

## Allowed claims

Within the included toy-model and reduced-surrogate setting, the repository may support the following bounded statements:

- endogenous stop-and-go waves can be reproduced in the included reduced stochastic ring-road surrogate;
- finite-capacity toy networks can display synchronization risk under shared or overly precise common information;
- risk-budgeted low-rate / coarse / staggered / guarded information release can improve mean cost without increasing overload severity in the tested frozen holdout setting;
- the results are hypothesis-generating diagnostics for synchronization, finite capacity, overload risk, and information-response coupling.

## Excluded claims

This repository does **not** claim:

- calibrated city-scale traffic prediction;
- validated real-world traffic policy;
- deployment-ready route guidance;
- public traffic-information control guidance;
- signal-control, ITS, navigation, or traffic-management product readiness;
- safety certification;
- a universal congestion solution;
- proof that information should generally be withheld;
- proof that real drivers, cities, or transport agencies should reduce public information access.

## Specific information-release boundary

The information-release result is limited to finite-capacity toy-network diagnostics. It should be read as a synchronization-risk hypothesis under tested surrogate conditions, not as a recommendation to withhold, suppress, delay, or manipulate real-world public traffic information.

Any real-world transportation, policy, navigation, safety, or public-information interpretation requires independent expert review, empirical validation, legal review, and institutional accountability outside the scope of this repository.
