# Claim Boundary

This repository is a public-gate archive for the Socio-Traffic Thermodynamics (STT) manuscript and its minimal numerical validation script.

## Supported limited claim

The archive supports the limited claim that a stochastic Optimal Velocity ring-road surrogate can exhibit spontaneous stop-and-go wave formation without a fixed exogenous bottleneck, consistent with the STT interpretation of congestion as a nonequilibrium dissipative structure.

## Not claimed

This archive does not claim:

- calibration to a real traffic network;
- real-world traffic forecasting accuracy;
- validated traffic-policy prescription;
- universal elimination of congestion;
- safety-critical traffic-control readiness;
- proof that all congestion phenomena arise from the same mechanism;
- replacement for established traffic-flow, transportation-planning, or network-routing models.

## Evidence level

The numerical evidence is a minimal stochastic ring-road demonstration. It is useful as a proof-of-concept consistency check, not as a broad empirical validation of STT across real cities or traffic systems.
