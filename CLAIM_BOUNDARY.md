# Claim Boundary

This repository is a public-gate archive for the Socio-Traffic Thermodynamics (STT) manuscript, its original minimal numerical validation script, and the v0.3.0 risk-budgeted information-release synthesis addendum.

## Supported limited claims

This archive supports only limited surrogate/toy-model claims.

1. A stochastic Optimal Velocity ring-road surrogate can exhibit spontaneous stop-and-go wave formation without a fixed exogenous bottleneck, consistent with the STT interpretation of congestion as a nonequilibrium dissipative structure.

2. Within the tested finite-capacity toy-network audit sequence, high-sensitivity, high-frequency, synchronized information response can amplify overload, oscillation, and social cost.

3. Within the frozen v0.2.9 holdout audit, a low-rate risk-budgeted information-release candidate improved mean cost relative to a no-information null without increasing overload severity.

These are hypothesis-generating and diagnostic claims within tested toy-model settings.

## Not claimed

This archive does not claim:

- calibration to a real traffic network;
- real-world traffic forecasting accuracy;
- validated traffic-policy prescription;
- city-scale deployment readiness;
- universal elimination of congestion;
- proof that information should generally be withheld;
- safety-critical traffic-control readiness;
- proof that all congestion phenomena arise from the same mechanism;
- replacement for established traffic-flow, transportation-planning, or network-routing models.

## Evidence level

The numerical evidence consists of a minimal stochastic ring-road demonstration and a staged finite-capacity toy-network audit sequence. It is useful as a proof-of-concept consistency check and hypothesis-generating diagnostic scaffold, not as broad empirical validation of STT across real cities or traffic systems.
