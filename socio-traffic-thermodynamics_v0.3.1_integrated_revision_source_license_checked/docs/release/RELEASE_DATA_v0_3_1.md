## Locked integrated result

The integrated interpretation is:

- endogenous stop-and-go wave formation can be reproduced in the reduced ring-road surrogate;
- shared precise or delayed common information can amplify synchronization, overload, and mean social cost in finite-capacity toy networks;
- risk-budgeted low-rate/coarse/staggered/guarded information release can improve mean cost while respecting overload-severity constraints in the tested frozen holdout;
- the result is a toy-model diagnostic, not real-world traffic-policy validation.

## Scope and claim boundary

This release does not claim:

- city-scale traffic prediction,
- policy validation,
- deployment-ready route guidance,
- traffic-management procurement readiness,
- safety certification,
- or a universal congestion solution.

The work should be read as a claim-bounded reduced-surrogate diagnostic for synchronization risk and risk-budgeted information release under finite capacity.
