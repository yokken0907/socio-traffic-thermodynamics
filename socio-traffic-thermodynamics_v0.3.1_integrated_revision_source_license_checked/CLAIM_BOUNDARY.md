# Claim boundary

This repository is a claim-bounded reduced-surrogate research package for Socio-Traffic Thermodynamics.

Allowed claims:

- endogenous stop-and-go waves can be reproduced in the included reduced stochastic ring-road surrogate;
- finite-capacity toy networks can display synchronization risk under shared information;
- risk-budgeted low-rate/coarse/staggered/guarded information release can improve mean cost without increasing overload severity in the tested frozen holdout;
- the results are hypothesis-generating diagnostics.

Excluded claims:

- calibrated city-scale traffic prediction;
- validated traffic policy;
- deployment-ready route guidance;
- universal congestion solution;
- safety-certified traffic-management product;
- proof that information should generally be withheld.
