# STT v0.3.0 Addendum Outline

## Provisional title

Risk-Budgeted Information Release in Finite-Capacity Traffic Networks: Claim-Bounded Toy Diagnostics and Frozen-Policy Holdout Tests

## Abstract direction

This addendum extends a claim-bounded socio-traffic thermodynamics framework by testing whether traffic-information release should be modeled as a synchronization-risk problem rather than as a pure information-precision problem. Across reduced finite-capacity network tests, synchronous high-rate common information can amplify overload and social cost. Frozen holdout diagnostics indicate that low-rate or coarse guidance can improve mean cost relative to a no-information baseline without increasing overload severity under the tested toy conditions. The result is hypothesis-generating and not a validated traffic policy.

## Key claim boundary sentence

The present results support only a reduced-model diagnostic claim: under the tested finite-capacity toy networks, low-rate or coarse information release can reduce mean social cost relative to no-information nulls without increasing overload severity, while high-rate synchronized common information can amplify overload and oscillation.
