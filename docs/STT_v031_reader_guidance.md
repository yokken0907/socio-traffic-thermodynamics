# Reader guidance for STT v0.3.1

Read this release as an integrated model-and-audit manuscript.

The original STT formulation supplies the conceptual and mathematical scaffold: social potential, finite bandwidth, finite capacity, stochastic car following, and dissipative stop-and-go wave formation.

The v0.2.x audit sequence narrows the actionable claim: information release should be interpreted as synchronization-control under finite capacity, not as simple precision maximization.

Do not interpret this release as a city-scale traffic model, policy recommendation, or route-guidance product.
