English: This manuscript integrates the earlier STT model formulation with the v0.2.0--v0.2.9 information-release audit sequence. It is not a real-world traffic-policy validation; it is a claim-bounded toy-model synthesis of information synchronization, overload risk, and mean social cost in finite-capacity networks.
